# 01 — Business and Commercial Blockers

This file covers what you need in order to sell, charge, and stay legal. These items are outside the Tenant features.

| # | Item | Priority | Evidence | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Trials never end.** Sign-up (`web-admin/app/register/tenant/page.tsx`) creates a "14-day free trial, no credit card". `processTrialExpirations()` in `web-admin/lib/services/subscriptions.service.ts:408` downgrades expired trials, but nothing calls it: there is no cron route, no HQ job and no worker. | Blocker | grep across both repos: only the function and its test reference it. **Confirmed live 2026-11-16:** a subscription with `trial_end = 2026-03-26` is still `status = trial`; 3 of 4 organizations are in trial. | Register it as an HQ ops job (`sys_ops_job_cf`) or a tenant cron route. Decide what "expired" means: downgrade to free, read-only, or suspend. The function also logs with `console.error`; switch it to the logger. |
| B2 | **No way to charge Tenants for the subscription.** HQ billing has plans, subscriptions, invoices, dunning and revenue screens. But `dunning.service.ts:239` and `:479` say "TODO: implement payment retry with payment gateway", and `:578` says "TODO: implement email sending". No card or gateway integration exists. | Blocker for self-serve; OK for a pilot | `cleanmatexsaas/platform-api/src/modules/billing/services/dunning.service.ts` | Pilot: invoice by hand from HQ and collect by bank transfer. Public launch: choose a GCC gateway that supports recurring cards (Tap, Checkout.com, PayTabs or Stripe where available), then build subscription checkout, auto-renew and dunning retry. |
| B3 | **The dunning reminder sends no email.** | Before public launch | `dunning.service.ts:578` | Send through the Notification Hub, which already exists. |
| B4 | **No legal pages.** There is no Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, cookie notice, Data Processing Agreement or acceptable-use policy in marketing-web or the tenant app. Sign-up does not record acceptance. | Blocker | grep for `privacy` / `terms` in `marketing-web/src` returns nothing. | Have a lawyer draft them for Oman (PDPL, Royal Decree 6/2022), Saudi Arabia (PDPL) and the UAE (PDPL). Publish them in EN/AR. Add an "I accept" checkbox to sign-up and store the version and time accepted. |
| B5 | **Data residency statement.** Customers' phone numbers and addresses sit in Supabase. Some GCC customers will ask where the data is stored. | Before public launch | — | Confirm the Supabase region and put it in the Privacy Policy. Check whether Saudi government or large B2B customers need in-country hosting. |
| B6 | **Support channel.** HQ PRD-0005 (Support & Ticketing) is at 0%. | Before public launch | HQ roadmap `new_roadmap_09032026/ROADMAP_PRIORITIZED_2026.md` | Pilot: a WhatsApp number plus email is enough. Later: a ticketing tool or the PRD. |
| B7 | **Pricing page versus real plans.** marketing-web has `/pricing`, and HQ has plans and feature flags per plan. They have not been checked against each other. | Before public launch | `marketing-web/src/app/[locale]/pricing/page.tsx` | Make the published prices, plan limits and flag mappings agree. Decide the currency (OMR, SAR or USD) and whether prices include VAT. |
| B8 | **CleanMateX's own VAT on its subscription invoices.** HQ invoices to Tenants need your VAT number and VAT when you pass the registration threshold. | Before public launch | — | Ask your tax adviser. Make sure HQ invoices show VAT correctly. |
| B9 | **Onboarding of a new Tenant.** Sign-up creates the organization, subscription and admin. After that the Tenant must confirm tax setup, add products and prices, set opening hours and holidays, and assign a workflow profile. Setup Health lists what is missing, but nothing has been tested end to end with a brand-new organization. | Blocker | Tax: "until an organization confirms its tax setup, its new orders carry no tax" (Tax `followups_by_user.md`). | Do one full "new Tenant from zero" run through sign-up → Setup Health all green → first order → first payment → first receipt. Write it up as a customer onboarding checklist. |
| B10 | **Marketing site** (`marketing-web`) has home, features, pricing, demo, contact and early-access pages. Its README is still the create-next-app default. Leads go to HQ `marketing-leads`. | Before public launch | — | Check EN/AR copy, the lead form reaching HQ, analytics and tracking consent (B4), and the domain and SSL. |

## Suggested order

B4 → B9 → B1 → pilot (with manual billing) → B2/B3 → B7/B8 → B5/B6/B10.

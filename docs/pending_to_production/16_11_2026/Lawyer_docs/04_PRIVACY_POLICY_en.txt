2. Two different roles
2.1 When we decide why and how data is used for our own business, we are the controller. That covers the marketing site, Organization accounts, staff logins, our invoices to laundries, our support mail, and our security logs.
2.2 When a laundry enters data about a person who brings clothes, the laundry is the controller and we are the processor. We use that data to provide the Service on the laundry's instructions. The Data Processing Agreement and the data-processing page describe that work.
2.3 If you are a person who only handed clothes to a laundry, start with the laundry. They decide the file, the messages, and the retention of the ticket. You may also write to [PRIVACY_EMAIL]. We will pass the request to the laundry and we will not change the laundry's records on our own, unless a binding legal duty requires us to act.

3. Data we collect as controller
3.1 Account data: the Organization's name, country, branch details, the name, email, phone, and login of staff users, authority statements, and the version and time of the Terms they accept.
3.2 Billing data: invoices we issue to the Organization, payment references for bank transfers, plan, and trial dates. The pilot does not collect a payment card.
3.3 Support data: messages you send to us, and what we need to investigate them.
3.4 Marketing-site data: the details you type in a contact or early-access form, and the technical data strictly necessary to serve the page.
3.5 Security data: IP address, user agent, and log events needed to protect accounts, for up to 12 months.
3.6 We do not ask you to send us a copy of a passport to start a pilot account. If a bank or a ministry later requires a know-your-customer document, we will tell you why, and we will not put that document in the laundry's customer notes.

4. Data we process for the laundry
4.1 On the laundry's instructions we process the End Customer's name, phone, email, address, language, order history, garment notes, prices, payments recorded at the counter, loyalty and wallet entries, and the communication choices recorded at order intake.
4.2 We process staff content that the laundry types into orders and settings.
4.3 A public order-tracking link, if the laundry uses one, shows the order status to anyone who has the link. The laundry should treat the link as confidential to the End Customer.
4.4 We do not sell End Customer lists. We do not use one laundry's customer list to advertise another laundry or to advertise CleanMateX to those people.

9. How long we keep data
9.1 Acceptance records: for the life of the Organization and 5 years after it ends.
9.2 Marketing leads: until you ask us to delete them, or 24 months after our last contact with you, whichever comes first.
9.3 Security logs that contain an IP address: 12 months.
9.4 Support correspondence: for the life of the account and 24 months after the last message, unless we need a longer copy for a live dispute.
9.5 End Customer data: for as long as the laundry instructs us to keep the file, and after anonymization in the reduced form described below.
9.6 Orders, payments, and issued tax documents: 10 years from the end of the financial year of the record, because the laundry must be able to keep its books. During that period the live file shows "Anonymized customer" once the laundry has used the anonymize action, and the phone, email, and address are removed from the working screens. A tax document that was already issued stays as it was issued.
9.7 When the period ends, we delete the record or keep it in a form that no longer identifies the person.

10. Your rights
10.1 If you are a staff user or a person who contacted us on the marketing site, you may ask us for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy, and you may object to processing that rests on legitimate interest. Where a processing rests on consent, you may withdraw that consent.
10.2 If you are an End Customer of a laundry, use the same rights with the laundry. The application gives the laundry an export and an anonymize action so they can answer you. Anonymize keeps the finance record as clause 9.6 describes. We assist the laundry. We do not silently rewrite their books.
10.3 We may ask for enough information to be sure we are dealing with the right person and the right Organization. We will not ask you to give us more identity documents than the request requires.
10.4 We aim to answer within 30 days. If the applicable law sets a shorter time, we use that shorter time.
10.5 You may complain to the authority in your country after you have written to us:
     Oman: the Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology.
     United Arab Emirates: the UAE Data Office.
     Bahrain: the Personal Data Protection Authority.
     Counsel confirms these names against the law in force on the day we publish.

11. Children
11.1 The Service is for businesses. We do not knowingly create an Organization account for a child.
11.2 A laundry must not open the contracting customer file in the name of a child. The adult who brings the clothes is the customer.

12. Security
12.1 We use access control, tenant separation, encryption in transit, and the encryption at rest provided by the hosting and database providers.
12.2 We do not claim a named certification in this version. If we later obtain one, we will say so in a new version of this policy.
12.3 No online service can promise that a breach is impossible. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs and the law requires notice, we will notify the laundry or the person, and the authority, within the required time.

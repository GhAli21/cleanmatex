Version: signup-2026-11-17
Language: English
Arabic pair: 14_SIGNUP_ACCEPTANCE_B4_ar.txt. That file is the legal translation of this text. The checkbox label in section 2 of that file is the Arabic label to show when the locale is ar.
Implements: roadmap B4 and Phase 3 step 3.2
Status: Decided. Use this wording. Do not shorten it in a way that drops the DPA or the authority sentence.

1. WHEN THE CHECKBOX APPEARS
On the screen that creates a new Organization (the Tenant sign-up).
Also on any later screen where a new authorized person re-accepts a new version of the Terms or the DPA.
The checkbox is unchecked when the screen loads.
The submit control does not create an Organization while the box is unchecked.
The links open the matching language of the pages in a form the person can read before ticking.

2. CHECKBOX WORDING
Label, next to the checkbox:

I have authority to bind this organization. I agree to the Terms of Service (tos-2026-11-17), the Data Processing Agreement (dpa-2026-11-17), and the Acceptable Use Policy (aup-2026-11-17), and I acknowledge the Privacy Policy (privacy-2026-11-17).

Link targets:
Terms of Service -> the Terms page
Data Processing Agreement -> the in-app or marketing page that shows 06_DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT_en.txt, or the public data-processing page plus the full DPA
Acceptable Use Policy -> the acceptable-use page
Privacy Policy -> the Privacy Policy page

Helper line under the checkbox, not a second checkbox:

The trial is 14 days. No payment card is collected on this form. Service messages to your customers are governed by the order-intake notice.

3. WHAT THE TICK MEANS
The tick records:
- the person states they can bind the Organization
- the Organization accepts the Terms, the DPA, and the Acceptable Use Policy of the stated versions
- the Organization acknowledges it has been shown the Privacy Policy of the stated version

The tick does not record:
- consent of any End Customer
- consent to marketing from CleanMateX
- consent to non-essential cookies
- a warranty that the person compared the Arabic and English texts

If the interface language is Arabic, store locale ar and show section 2 of 14_SIGNUP_ACCEPTANCE_B4_ar.txt.
If the interface language is English, store locale en and show section 2 of this file.
Both language texts are part of the accepted version.

4. RECORD THE SERVER MUST STORE
Create the row in the same transaction that creates the Organization, or immediately after, before the user can enter end-customer data.
If the row cannot be stored, roll back the sign-up and show an error. Do not leave an Organization with no acceptance.

Fields:
tenant_org_id          the new Organization
user_id                the person who ticked
acceptance_method      SIGNUP_CHECKBOX
pack_version           legal-pack-2026-11-17
tos_version            tos-2026-11-17
dpa_version            dpa-2026-11-17
aup_version            aup-2026-11-17
privacy_version        privacy-2026-11-17
signup_copy_version    signup-2026-11-17
accepted_at            UTC timestamp from the server clock
locale                 en or ar
checkbox_was_preselected   false
source_ip              the request IP if the application already logs it for security; otherwise null
user_agent             the request user agent if already available; otherwise null
authority_confirmed    true

Do not store the password in this row.
Do not store a copy of the whole Terms in the row. The version code points to the text in this folder, and the published page for that code must remain retrievable.

Keep the row for the life of the Organization and for 5 years after it ends.

5. LATER VERSIONS
When a version code changes, the next admin login shows the new text and a new unchecked box.
Continuing to use the Service after that screen requires a new row.
Existing rows stay.
Access to historical orders does not require re-acceptance if the Owner decides a maintenance window must stay open, but creating new orders can be held until the new tick is stored. The pilot default is: hold new orders until the new tick is stored; keep read access to existing records.

6. MARKETING LEADS
The early-access and contact forms are not this checkbox.
Their English submit line is:

By sending this form you ask CleanMateX to contact you about the Service. The Privacy Policy (privacy-2026-11-17) explains how that request is handled.

The Arabic submit line is section 6 of 14_SIGNUP_ACCEPTANCE_B4_ar.txt.

Store the lead with the privacy version and the time.
That store is not an Organization acceptance and does not create a tenant.

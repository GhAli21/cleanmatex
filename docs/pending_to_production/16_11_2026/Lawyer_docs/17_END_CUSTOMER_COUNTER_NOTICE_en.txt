Who holds your file
This laundry keeps your name and contact details so it can take your order, do the work, and record the payment. The laundry uses CleanMateX software to keep that file. CleanMateX hosts the file for the laundry and does not use it to advertise other shops to you.

Updates
If you give a mobile number or an email, the laundry uses it to send updates needed for this order, such as ready, receipt, and delivery. You may say no to SMS and no to email.
WhatsApp is sent only if you tick yes.
Offers are sent only if you tick yes to offers. You can refuse every message and still leave the clothes.

How long
The laundry keeps the order and the payment record for its accounts and its tax, for up to 10 years from the end of the financial year. If you ask the laundry to erase you, it removes your phone, email, and address from the working file and keeps the money record. A receipt already issued stays as it was issued.

Your choices
You can ask this laundry for a copy of your file, for a correction, or for erasure. You can change message choices with the laundry at any time.

Who holds your file
This Tenant keeps your name and contact details so it can take your order, do the work, and record the payment. The Tenant uses CleanMateX software to keep that file. CleanMateX hosts the file for the Tenant and does not use it to advertise other shops to you.

Updates
If you give a mobile number or an email, the Tenant uses it to send updates needed for this order, such as ready, receipt, and delivery. You may say no to SMS and no to email.
WhatsApp is sent only if you tick yes.
Offers are sent only if you tick yes to offers. You can refuse every message and still leave the clothes.

How long
The Tenant keeps the order and the payment record for its accounts and its tax, for up to 10 years from the end of the financial year. If you ask the Tenant to erase you, it removes your phone, email, and address from the working file and keeps the money record. A receipt already issued stays as it was issued.

Your choices
You can ask this Tenant for a copy of your file, for a correction, or for erasure. You can change message choices with the Tenant at any time.

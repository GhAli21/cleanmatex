- Omani company: laws of the Sultanate of Oman; courts of Muscat.

4. RETENTION FIGURES USED IN THE PUBLISHED TEXTS
Accounting and tax records of an Organization, and the issued tax documents of that Organization's sales: 10 years from the end of the financial year of the record.
This 10-year period is the pilot safe harbor across Oman, the UAE, and Bahrain.
A shorter period replaces it only when the tax adviser writes the shorter period into Tax_Config/KIT_FACTS.md and this register is updated to a new pack version in both languages.
Backups: overwritten or expired on the cycle in [BACKUP_RETENTION]. Selective deletion inside a live backup is not promised.
Sign-up acceptance records: kept for the life of the account and for 5 years after the account ends.
Order-intake consent records: kept for the life of the customer record and for 5 years after anonymization or account end.
Server security logs that contain an IP address: 12 months, then delete or aggregate.
Marketing-site lead forms: kept until the person asks for deletion, or for 24 months after the last contact, whichever comes first.

5. CHANGE CONTROL
A change to a versioned document requires:
- a new version code
- the new text in both languages
- a note in this register, in both language files, of what changed and why
- a fresh acceptance where the change is material to the Organization's duties or to the processing
A change that only corrects a bracket with the matching real fact, before any customer has accepted the version, may keep the same code if no customer has seen it. The same fact is written into both language files.
After the first real acceptance, any change uses a new code.

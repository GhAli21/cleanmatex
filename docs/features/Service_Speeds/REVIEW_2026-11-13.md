## 2. Gaps (missing pieces)

- **G1** No cross-field checks on the calendar: a cut-off after closing, a ready time before opening, or a ready time earlier than the cut-off all save without warning.
- **G2** The Branches tab is not gated by `branches:read`. A user without it gets "Could not load the calendar". A failed branch list shows an empty select.
- **G3** Public holidays are seeded for 2026–2027 only. After 2027 the engine silently ignores holidays. There is no Setup Health check ("no holidays for the next 12 months") and no year hint in the Holidays tab.
- **G4** A branch cannot say "no cut-off" (null always inherits). Overnight hours are not supported (by design, but undocumented). Standard cannot use working days.
- **G5** The customer app cannot see per-speed price or ready time before booking (owner decision F7). After `SPEED_NOT_OFFERED` it does not return the customer to the speed step.
- **G6** Missing tests: 24:xx stored hours, a branch with every day closed, working days beyond the scan limit, a 24:00 holiday, an impossible date, product-hours fallback for a fast speed, bulk SELL_PRICE below product price.

## 3. Enhancements (ease of use, future-proofing)

- **E1** A "Test: an order placed now" card on Business Calendar: ready time per speed for this branch, using the same engine.
- **E2** A "Reset to country template" button and a quick weekly pattern. Show the template, and the time zone next to every time field (including a branch's own zone).
- **E3** Holidays: "Close all public holidays for this branch", a filter (own / public), "We open this day" prefilled with that weekday's hours, and an "overridden" badge.
- **E4** Speed menu: show the date for multi-day ready times (not just "Sun 6:00 PM"). Put the disabled reason in `aria-describedby` and wrap money and time in `<bdi dir="ltr">`.
- **E5** Speed Pricing: show the cut-off the rule overrides ("Currently 11:00, organization"). Make the percent hints use the app locale.
- **E6** HQ: a bulk "mark confirmed" action on estimated public holidays. Derive `weekend_days` from the hours.
- **E7** Optimistic concurrency (`updated_at`) on calendar PUTs.
